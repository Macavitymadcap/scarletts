# Photo

A band photo in a `border-hard` `ink` frame, upright and square cut, with an optional typewritten credit and an optional `Sticker` on the top-right corner.

- Always pass `width` and `height` (from `photos.ts`) so the page reserves space while the image loads.
- `alt` describes who and what is in the shot: "Scarletts on stage at a Bristol pub".
- Photos sit flush in a grid with no gaps; put a `scarlet` block beside a black and white shot for the 2 Tone look.
